# Privacy Policy — Uni Tonight

**Effective Date:** October 18, 2026

**Uni Tonight** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of university students using our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account & Profile Information:** When you sign up using your university email address (`.ac.uk` or `.edu`), we collect your email address, display name, bio, night-out vibe preferences, and optional profile photos via Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.
- **User-Generated Content (Crews & Plans):** When you create or join a night-out crew, your crew details and participation are visible to other signed-in university students in the app.
- **Location Information:** With your explicit permission, we use your approximate or precise location to display nearby student venues and night-out hotspots on the interactive map. You can revoke location permissions at any time in iOS Settings.
- **Advertising & Identifiers (App Tracking Transparency):** We use Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. If you grant permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt, device identifiers and ad interaction data may be used by Google AdMob to deliver relevant ads. If you decline tracking, only non-personalized ads are shown.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- To verify university student status and authenticate your account.
- To display live student crews, events, and nearby nightlife venues.
- To maintain community safety and support app functionality.
- To serve advertisements via Google AdMob.

## 3. Data Sharing & Storage
- Your data is securely stored using **Google Firebase** (Authentication & Cloud Firestore).
- We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

## 4. Data Retention & Account Deletion
- You may update your profile at any time inside the **Profile** tab.
- To request complete deletion of your account and all associated data, contact us or submit a deletion request via our support page, and your data will be permanently removed within 7 days.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact **Samuel Morrison** via the [Uni Tonight Support Page](https://github.com/samuelmorrison99/uni-tonight-support).
