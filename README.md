# Uni Tonight — Official App Support

Welcome to the official support page for **Uni Tonight** (iOS).

## About Uni Tonight
Uni Tonight helps verified university students discover student-friendly venues, exclusive drink deals, and connect with fellow students for nights out across campus and the city.

## Contact & Support
If you need help with your account, university email verification, reporting content or users, or deleting your account, please contact us:
